Women Trailblazers in Tech Leadership

At Curious Cardinals, we believe that representation matters. That’s why we’re celebrating this Women’s History Month by featuring four female trailblazers in technology: Deb Liu, CEO of Ancestry; Emily White, Partner at Anthos Capital; Lexi Reese, former COO of Gusto; and Varsha Rao, CEO of Nurx. These women have paved the way for others to defy the norm and become influential leaders in tech. Co-founder & CEO Audrey, a first time female founder in tech herself, will facilitate a discussion with these revolutionary female leaders. They'll discuss what representation means to them and offer advice they wish their younger selves could have heard, particularly in historically male dominated industries. We hope to share this with as many individuals as possible, particularly young women, to inspire them to pursue their dreams, even if they feel underrepresented in their journey!
