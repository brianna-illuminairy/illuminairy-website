Structure & Flexibility

Option 1

Intensive Project Launch

Bring a bold idea to life — and make it matter. Students will launch a project designed for real-world impact, whether it’s generating revenue, growing a user base, getting published, or driving change in their community. They’ll walk away with a finished product, public proof of work, and a story worth sharing.
Schedule: 20-hour package, typically 2 hours per week for 10 weeks.
Option 2

Flexible Monthly Subscription

Develop an impactful Minimum Viable Product (MVP) by summer's end, structured to continue scaling into the school year.
Schedule: Meet 4 hours per month, with an initial 2-week evaluation period to align on goals and determine if you’d like to move forward.
  • 2-week evaluation period to ensure you’re happy with your mentor. (Sessions billed at $95/hr during evaluation). Once you’re subscribed, there is a 2-month commitment.
  • Roll over up to 3 hours of unused credits monthly. Cancel or pause anytime after the initial commitment.
  • 100% money-back guarantee: We’re confident in the value we provide, so if you’re not completely satisfied after 2 weeks of working with Curious Cardinals, we’ll give you a full refund — no questions asked
Option 3

Pathfinding Bootcamp

Turn confusion into clarity. In this short-term intensive, your child will uncover what excites them most, map out a clear 12-month plan for a passion project, and build timeless skills like self-awareness, storytelling, and purpose-driven learning.
Schedule: Flexible intensive (1 hour/day for 1-2 weeks), designed to fit into gaps in your busy summer schedule.
