How it works

Schedule a consultation
Schedule a free 30 minute zoom call with our family admissions team (Harvard & Stanford Graduate School of Education alum and former educators). We will discuss your student’s interests, academic profile, and goals. If your student is around, we encourage them to join the call. 
Receive a personalized learning plan
After we’ve aligned on what success looks like for your student, we will recommend a personalized learning roadmap that provides clear structure to their learning. We will also provide examples of tangible outcomes from previous students to inspire your family's vision.
Review mentor recommendation
We are expert match makers. We will identify a mentor profile that not only shares multiple nodes of connection with your student and has a track record of delivering results. 
‍
98% of our customers love their mentor match (students literally light up when they see an older aspirational version of themself), but if you’re not thrilled, we’ll keep trying until we get it right.
Begin evaluation & see results
Your student will meet with their mentor virtually on a flexible weekly schedule. You’ll receive  notes after each session as well as a structured roadmap with real-time progress tracking. You will also receive seasonal progress reports that clearly captures your student’s achievements, skills acquired, and tangible deliverables.
