MICHAEL SEIBEL

Michael Seibel is CEO of Y Combinator, one of the world's leading startup accelerators, where he has helped thousands of early-stage companies launch and scale. Before joining YC, he co-founded Twitch and was acquired by Amazon for nearly $1 billion.

Laura Modi is the CEO and founder of Bobbie, the first woman-owned organic infant formula company in the U.S., which she started in 2018 after struggling to find clean formula for her own children. Her company reached $100 million in revenue within 18 months and now feeds over 4.5% of U.S. babies.

ZARNA GARG

Zarna Garg is an Indian-American comedian who started her stand-up career in her late forties and became a breakout star with her sharp humor about immigrant life and motherhood. Her relatable material about cultural identity has amassed a social media following of over 2 million.
