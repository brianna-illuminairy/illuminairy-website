Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if my student doesn’t connect with their mentor?
We pride ourselves on finding the best person in the world to help your student achieve their goals. After the first session, we will check in to make sure your student feels like it was a great match. If not, they can fire their mentor! Ha, you can never show up to the first day of school and fire your teacher if you don’t like them. At Curious Cardinals, students have agency to choose not just what they learn, but also whom they learn from.
What is an academic mentor?

Our mentors are a mix between a student’s best friend and favorite teacher. Having a cool, relatable academic mentor will solve all of your problems. Does your student not listen to you? Tired of repeating yourself and playing the “bad cop”? That’s what an academic mentor is for!

What is a student profile?

We create a student profile with you during the consultation call to keep up with your student’s progress and goals. It is meant to capture their personality and interests, qualities a student would want in their dream mentor, with a learning proposal, and a clear definition of success for their engagement with Curious Cardinals. We use the student profile to “pitch” their prospective mentor on the opportunity to work with your student. This allows us to properly set expecations so the mentor specifically knows how to best support your student and think of it almost as a contract that Curious Cardinals and their mentor are held accountable to fulfill.
