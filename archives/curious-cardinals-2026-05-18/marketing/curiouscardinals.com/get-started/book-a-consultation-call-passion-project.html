Founder letter

I still remember staring at that blank piece of paper.

It was my freshman year, and I had just been cut from the academy soccer team. My "perfect" plan was shattered. Like many students, I had no idea what made me unique.

Sound familiar?

Maybe your child is:

▸ Getting good grades but missing that "spark" 
▸ Struggling to find their "thing" that makes them stand out 
▸ Doing all the right activities but lacking passion 
▸ Following the standard playbook but blending in with thousands of others

Here's what nobody tells you: Real standout achievements don't come from following a template. They come from finding that sweet spot where passion meets impact.

I discovered this by accident. That blank paper became Soles4Good — a nonprofit that's now transformed lives across multiple countries. Not because I was trying to "build my resume," but because I found something that truly mattered to me.

Today, I watch the same transformation happen with our students:  
▸ A gaming enthusiast who built an 8,000-download educational app 
▸ A fashion lover who launched a sustainable clothing movement 
▸ A coding beginner who created accessibility software for their grandmother

These aren't just impressive achievements. They're authentic stories that set students apart. That's why we built our Passion Project program at Curious Cardinals.

We pair your child with extraordinary mentors from Harvard, Stanford, MIT, and Yale who:

▸ Have built their own successful ventures 
▸ Know how to turn interests into impact 
▸ Understand what top schools really look for 
▸ Can guide your child from idea to execution

Because here's the truth: Colleges don't want to see another "volunteer project" or "leadership position." They want to see what makes your child uniquely them.

Your child has that spark. They just need the right guidance to turn it into something extraordinary.

Ready to help them stand out?
