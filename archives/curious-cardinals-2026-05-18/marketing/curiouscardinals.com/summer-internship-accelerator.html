Our 10-Session Framework:
The Blueprint to Success

1

Session 1: Discovery & Interest Mapping

Uncover your genuine interests and create your personalized internship roadmap using our proven framework that identifies where passion meets opportunity.

2

Session 2: Network & Opportunity Mapping

Build your comprehensive target list of 50+ organizations while learning to leverage connections you didn't even realize you had.

3

Session 3: Resume Development Workshop

Transform your experiences into a compelling professional narrative that showcases your unique value to potential employers.

4

Session 4: Resume Finalization & Outreach Prep

Perfect your resume and develop customized outreach templates that will get noticed by busy professionals.

5

Session 5: First Outreach Launch

Execute your initial outreach campaign with strategic messaging that stands out from generic inquiries.

6

Session 6: Informational Interview Preparation

Master the art of the informational interview with research techniques and elevator pitch development that makes you memorable.

7

Session 7: Second Round of Outreach

Refine your approach based on early results and launch your second wave of strategic outreach to secure multiple options.

8

Session 8: Deep Interview Preparation

Prepare for formal interviews with mock sessions and targeted research that positions you as exceptionally prepared and professional.

9

Session 9: Opportunity Assessment

Evaluate your options using our decision matrix framework to select the internship that best aligns with your goals.

10

Session 10: Internship Success Strategies

Learn professional excellence tactics, including "managing up," documentation systems, and relationship-building strategies that turn a summer experience into long-term connections.

Important Note

Success in this program requires commitment to weekly homework assignments. Students who complete all assigned work between sessions have dramatically higher success rates in securing meaningful internships.
