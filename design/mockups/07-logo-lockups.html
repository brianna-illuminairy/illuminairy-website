Rules (from your feedback)

Do

  • Single-color “Illuminairy” wordmark
  • Stars / arc as the icon story (v7)
  • Source Serif headlines + Hanken body
  • On-dark + on-light SVG variants

Don't

  • Split illumin / airy colors
  • v2 path + gold “ai”
  • Mechanical line-cross stars (v7 SVG)
  • Cormorant in funnel / enroll / portal
  • Mix v2 path mark with aurora stars
