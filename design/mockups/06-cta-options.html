Hover motion (you probably remember this)

Fill color and hover behavior are separate choices. The “glow on hover” you liked is not in the quiz funnel today — it lives on LP v4 and enroll checkout buttons.

Glow lift + arrow Button rises 1px, colored shadow blooms, arrow slides right. Mint/aurora shadow even when fill is forest or celestial. LP v4 · enroll · aurora-btn-primary
Glow lift on mint fill Same motion on the old mint pill — feels good on hover but still weak at rest on light pages. .std-paybtn · .lp-btn (current)
Flat darken only No shadow bloom — just background shift. What Plan Builder uses now. Quiz funnel · .qf-btn
Border-glow ring Conic aurora ring spins around the pill on hover — brand guide squares, adapted for CTAs. Brand guide · .visual-card cousin
Pulse ring Soft expanding halo on hover — use sparingly (hero / one CTA per page). New option · optional
CTA card top-line Often paired with the button above — whole card lifts, celestial→aurora line sweeps in. Hover the card. LP v4 · .lp-cta-card
