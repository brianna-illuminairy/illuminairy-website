Brand voice

Calm Intelligent Encouraging Editorial Modern Wonder-driven

Do

  • Light #F5F8FA page backgrounds
  • Soft glow CTA pills on light cards
  • Aurora hover lines on cards and rows
  • Slim navy header + footer only
  • One company feel across LP → funnel → enroll

Don't

  • Beige / warm-gray portal backgrounds (use polar white)
  • Navy CTA boxes above the fold
  • Full-page dark mode / coder aesthetic
  • Hanken 800 + all-caps mono labels
  • Fraunces / Cormorant on product UI
