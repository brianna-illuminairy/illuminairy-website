Quick take

If you want serif in the logo: try space after “Ill” or Literata/Lora/Baskerville before giving up on serif.

If pronunciation matters most in the header: Hanken wordmark (sans) + Source Serif headlines on the page — clearest Ill, still editorial below the fold.

Uppercase tracked Hanken removes the Ill problem entirely but feels more SaaS chrome than magazine.
