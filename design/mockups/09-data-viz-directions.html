7 · Current → target · dumbbell vs horizon

Same data, two ways to show the gap: a horizontal dumbbell (gap size) vs a two-point horizon path (forward momentum). Pick one for plan reveal / LP hero; use full horizon (§1) when milestones matter.

Option A · Dumbbell

The gap is the story

Best when: parent needs to see exactly how far today is from the goal in one glance. Works in narrow funnel column. Feels like a measured distance, not a stock chart.

Option B · Horizon (2-point)

The path is the story

Best when: you want aurora brand energy and forward motion. Same two endpoints as the dumbbell, but reads as a journey. Add milestones (§1) when the plan has PT weeks.

How to choose: Dumbbell for interstitials and tight mobile (gap clarity). Two-point horizon for LP hero and plan card header (brand warmth). Never use side-by-side vertical bars. If the view includes week 4 / week 8 checkpoints, use the full horizon path from §1 instead of either option here.
