## Voice rules

- **Complete sentences** — no telegram hooks (“3.8 GPA. 1180 SAT. 1400 on the list.”).
- **Say the colleges out loud** — **“the colleges they’re applying to expect SAT scores around 1400.”** Never **“the list,”** **“on the list,”** or **“1400 on the list”** (parents hear a riddle).
- **Parent words:** **AP classes**, GPA, Khan, Bluebook, fall SAT — not “honors track,” “score band,” “move the needle.”
- **Hooks:** don't trash **apps** or **tutors** — different things. Apps *do* show missed questions; parents want **whether the score can move** and **what to study**. Save Khan/tutor mentions for **body copy** (what they already tried), not hook punchlines.
- **Chain every primary:** situation → why usual prep fails → what **2-min Improvement Plan** gives → **Strategy Call** → **Skill Diagnostic** → tutoring on ranked misses.
- **Gap, not plateau** (Scripts 1,2,4,5,6): score **too low for the colleges they’re targeting** (e.g. 1200s, colleges expect ~1400) — most parents have **one** score so far.

| Don’t write | Write instead |
|-------------|----------------|
| 1400 on the list | The colleges they’re applying to expect SAT scores around 1400 |
| List needs 1400 | Her SAT was 1240; schools like UGA and Georgia Tech want closer to 1400 *(use their real targets when you can)* |
| School should tell us which SAT questions he misses | The SAT is outside school — **we had no plan for the fall test** / **we didn't know what to study** |
| Apps don't show wrong answers / "which problems kept coming back" | Apps show misses — don't claim otherwise. **Hooks:** skip app-bashing; use **score stuck after studying** |
| Tutors vs apps (1:1 comparison) | Don't compare — say **what we tried** (body only) or **score still didn't move** |
| What to fix first / work on first / vague "what to study" | **Highest-impact skills to focus on** so the score improves **as fast as possible** |
| What to do differently (alone) | Pair with **highest-impact skills** — that's the mechanism, not a slogan |
| Wasting money / time, tutor #4, before we paid for… | Don't plant loss — say **a plan that could move the score**, **what to study**, **realistic target** |
| Timeline / "nobody gave us weeks until…" | Parents **know the test date** — they need **how to use the time**, **what to study**, **whether the score can still move** |
| Buying another course / paying for tutor #N | **Same study loop, same score** / **what to do differently this time** |
| 1200s, list near 1400 | SAT in the 1200s, but target colleges expect scores around 1400 |
| Two SATs in / wasting the fall | After **two official SAT scores**, we didn't know if she could **still raise the score enough before application deadlines** |
| "Is 1400 realistic for her colleges?" (Script 3) | **Can she still improve enough on the fall SAT before apps go in** (EA **November 1**) — and **what to do differently this time** |
- **Script 3 only:** child took SAT **twice+**; parent asks **can the score still move enough before application deadlines** (not “is 1400 realistic for School X”).
- Stats: **+182**, n=**95**, **78%** hit target — `lib/site.ts` only; tie to **diagnostic-first**. Footer: Results vary (not in hook).

## Competitor ads intel (June 2026)

Source: competitor UGC/transcript batch (16 ads) — full text in [`competitor-ads-transcripts-2026-06.md`](./competitor-ads-transcripts-2026-06.md). Use for **positioning contrast**, not paste.

### What they all do (category noise)

| Pattern | Examples | Illuminairy |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| **GPA/SAT mismatch** | Ads 1, 8, 12 — “A student, stuck 1100–1200s” | **Script 1** — same situation, our voice |
| **Diagnostic → custom plan → 1:1** | Ads 1, 4, 7, 10, 14 | Same arc — but we lead with **free Improvement Plan** (~2 min, parent only), then **Strategy Call**, then **Skill Diagnostic** |
| **“Few things that move score most”** | Ads 1, 10, 15 — highest yield / 12–15 concepts | Our line: **highest-impact skills**, score improves **fastest** (5–6 after diagnostic) |
| **Score stuck after 2–3 tries** | Ads 11, 14 | **Script 3** — can it still move before apps? |
| **May/June score → fall/apps** | Ads 16 | **Scripts 5–6** |
| **Student UGC overwhelmed** | Ad 5 (Valentina) | Optional creator angle — AP load + focused plan |

### Do not copy (banned or off-brand for us)

| Their move | Why skip |
|------------|----------|
| **Score guarantees** (1500, +140, refund) | Ads 4, 7, 9, 10, 13, 15 — we never guarantee |
| **“Costing / losing points,” patterns costing points** | Ads 1, 7, 12, 14 — tutor-ad jargon (`messaging-guide`) |
| **Khan/content is the problem** | Ads 1, 4 — too blunt; quiz uses **breadth / haystack** only after q7, not hooks |
| **Ivy / Top 20 / BSMD / 1500 minimum** | Ads 6–10, 13 — we say **colleges they're applying to expect ~1400** |
| **Fake scarcity** (“5 students/month”) | Ads 1, 4, 9 — skip |
| **“SAT is strategy/pattern test not academic”** | Ads 1, 2, 8, 12 — oversimplified; we use **school math ≠ test-day math** (script 1 / q4 hit) |
| **Book free assessment/diagnostic first** | Almost all — our CTA is **Build their SAT Improvement Plan** → `/plan?step=q1` |

### Hook takeaway

Competitors open on **the same parent situations** we already have in the hooks table. They **sell the consultation** in the hook; we should **sell the situation** in the hook and the **free plan + highest-impact skills** in Primary A.

**Closest competitor hook (Ad 12)** — situation only, no guarantee:

> High GPA, but low SAT… usually means no one has shown them **where to focus** so the score moves.

Our version stays concrete: **1180**, colleges expect **~1400**, **which skills would move his score fastest** — not “bad at test-taking?”
