Aha moments

Skills that improved in sessions

  • Systems with opposite slopes — same intercept and opposite slopes cross exactly once; no need to set equations equal blindly.
  • Nonlinear with a constant (y − 42) — the question asks for possible y values, not c; cannot plug into Desmos when c is unknown; factor by hand.
  • Boundaries — label each side as full clause vs fragment; do not rely on “sounds right” pauses.
  • Transitions — read before and after the blank, name the relationship (contrast, addition, cause-and-effect), then match a word; barren vs fertile flagged a contrast miss.
  • M1Q11 on the whiteboard — split on operators, factor (y − 42) with a placeholder 1; do not cancel shared terms.
  • 3x + k factor problem — factor GCF 3x first to read k from the factored form.
  • FOIL when both forms are given — match F, O+I, and L to solve for unknown constants.
