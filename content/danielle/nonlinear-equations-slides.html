Nonlinear equations and systems

Your most common Math question type. The skill is reading it, then knowing exactly how to solve it.

Danielle Danso · Recognition

Move with → ← or the buttons. On the practice cards, answer out loud first, then reveal.
The routine, every question

Read it, then make three calls

  • Name the type. Which of the five kinds is this?
  • Calculator or by hand?
  • Name the approach. Factor, quadratic formula, clear the fraction, square both sides, substitute.

Only reach for the calculator if it can actually give the answer. A letter constant or an exact form means by hand.

The five subtypes

What lives in this domain

  • ✶Absolute value: bars around an expression, |2x − 3| = 7.
  • ✶Quadratic: a squared variable, x² − 10x + 7 = 0.
  • ✶Rational: a variable in a denominator, 1 / (x² + 6x + 9) = 3.
  • ✶Other nonlinear: a radical or a higher power, √(x + 5) = x − 1.
  • ✶System, nonlinear and linear: a line and a curve, y = 2x and y = x² − 3.
Subtype 1 of 5

Absolute value equations

Absolute value bars around an expression.

RecognizeBars around the expression, like |2x − 3| = 7.
Calc or handBy hand. Splitting is faster and exact.
ApproachIsolate the bars, then set the inside equal to the positive and the negative of the other side. Solve both.
GotchaIf the absolute value equals a negative number, there is no solution.
Subtype 2 of 5

Quadratic equations

A squared variable is the highest power.

RecognizeAn x² term. Asks for solutions, the number of solutions, the sum or product of roots, or a solution in a special form.
Calc or handBy hand when there is a letter constant or an exact form. Calculator only for a clean numeric root.
ApproachPick one of five tools: factor, sum and product of roots, discriminant, quadratic formula plus simplify, or hidden structure.
GotchaA parameter or an "a + b√n" answer means the calculator cannot finish it. See the factoring and radical decks.
Subtype 3 of 5

Rational equations

A variable sits in a denominator.

RecognizeA fraction with a variable on the bottom, set equal to something, like 1 / (x² + 6x + 9) = 3.
Calc or handBy hand. Clear the fraction.
ApproachMultiply both sides by the denominator to clear it, then solve what is left (often a quadratic).
GotchaAny value that makes a denominator zero is not allowed. Check answers against that.
Subtype 4 of 5

Other nonlinear equations

Not linear, and not a plain quadratic. A radical or a higher power.

RecognizeA square root of x, an x³, or a variable in an exponent.
Calc or handGraph for "how many solutions" or a numeric answer. By hand for an exact answer.
ApproachIsolate, then undo the operation. Square both sides for a radical, factor for a polynomial, or graph and read.
GotchaSquaring both sides can add fake solutions. Always check them in the original equation.
Subtype 5 of 5

System: nonlinear and linear

Two equations, one a line and one a curve.

RecognizeA line and a curve (a parabola or a circle), asked where they meet.
Calc or handGraphing is often fastest. By hand, use substitution.
ApproachSolve the line for one variable, substitute into the curve, solve the resulting quadratic. Or graph both and read the intersections.
GotchaThere can be 0, 1, or 2 solutions. If asked, give both coordinates of each point.
