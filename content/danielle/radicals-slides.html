The idea

What it means

Simplifying a square root means pulling any perfect-square factor out of it.

Perfect squares to know on sight:

4916253649 6481100121144
The method

Three steps, worked on √72

  • ✶Find the largest perfect square that divides 72. That is 36.
  • ✶Split the root: √72 = √(36 · 2) = √36 · √2.
  • ✶Take the square root of 36 out front: 6√2.

The whole move

Finishing the quadratic formula

Simplify the radical, then divide every term by the bottom number.

  • ✶Her Q4: x = (10 ± √72) / 2 → (10 ± 6√2) / 2 → 5 ± 3√2, so n = 2.
  • ✶Her #18: x = (6 ± √20) / 2 → (6 ± 2√5) / 2 → 3 ± √5, so n = 5.
Watch for these

Common mistakes

PitfallNot simplifying all the way. √72 = 2√18 is unfinished, because 18 still hides a perfect square. Pull out the largest one.
Her Q4 errorMatching before dividing by the bottom number. She set 3√n = √72, got n = 8, and never simplified or divided by 2.
PitfallDividing only one term. Every term gets divided by the bottom number.
