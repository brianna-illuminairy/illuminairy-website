Aha moments

Where it clicked in our sessions

  • She names the relationship before she reads the choices. Cover the blank, say in plain words what sentence 2 is doing to sentence 1, then match. That replaced guessing from the answer list.
  • Copycat elimination. When two answer choices do the same job (both contrast, both cause), she crosses both out before rereading the passage. That cut down wrong picks on medium questions.
  • Wallace Stevens (diagnostic easy miss). She walked the contrast method and landed on Nonetheless once she labeled sentence 2 as pushing back on sentence 1.
  • Cosmic Canvas (diagnostic medium miss). She reframed it as sequence: first impression (colorful nebulae) → then the subtitle grounds the viewer in ordinary paint swatches. First impression to final understanding.
  • Rhythmicity study (diagnostic hard miss). She mapped it as concession: sentence A says rhythmicity helps mastery; sentence B admits that link is weaker than melody complexity. Granted fit once she named that partial walk-back.
  • Flashcards locked the word buckets. After sorting contrast, causation, addition, and similarity in session, she hit 96% on the flashcard deck the same week.
