Aha moments

Skills that improved in sessions

  • Naming the relationship before reading choices — contrast vs addition vs soft cause-and-effect, instead of picking what sounds right in the blank.
  • Soft cause-and-effect — Yeats’s mysticism influenced symbolic poetry; swap in “as a result” vs “in the same way” to test.
  • Tip-off words — despite/even though → contrast; as a result/because of → cause-and-effect; also/another → addition; time cues → sequence.
  • Module 1 transition miss — slow-down language plus opposite outcomes → in contrast, not for example.
  • y − 42 factoring — move to one side, split components, factor (y − 42) with a placeholder 1; do not cancel shared terms.
  • 3x + k factor problem — factor GCF 3x first (not x alone) to read k = 4 from the factored form.
