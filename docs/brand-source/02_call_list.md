## Targeting rules

- **70% / 30% mix** — 70% solo professionals or operators inside a small team; 30% small-business owners (the dentist archetype).
- **Avoid the choir** — skip anyone who already knows IlluminAIry exists. Their politeness will pollute the signal.
- **Reach forward, not back** — recent contacts > distant contacts. People who've actively tried to apply AI in the last 6 months > people who "should care."
- **Mix verticals** — don't fill all 15 with one persona. We're picking a beachhead in Module 4, so let the data drive it.
- **Cold-ask language:** *"Can I get 20 minutes to learn about your experience trying to apply AI to your work? I'm doing research, not selling anything."*
