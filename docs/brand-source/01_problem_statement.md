## v4

Businesses and professionals who can't turn AI into actual outcomes will get left behind by the ones who can. AI enablement is no longer optional. IlluminAIry provides the plan and the guide to implement AI.

There is no fast path from "I know I should be using AI for my career and business" to "I achieved my outcome with AI". Search traffic for 'How do I use AI to [XYZ] in my [career/business]' has exploded. So have searches for 'AI Coach'. Demand for both the outcome AND the guide is surging, but there's nobody that connects the two.

Courses are generic and static and go stale before you finish them. Freelancer marketplaces give you output without context so you can never do it yourself. Gurus sell hype but provide generic trainings that aren't relevant to specific use cases.

So most ambitious professionals and business owners stall. They're using AI, but to do what? To produce more documents faster and AI generated single-use content? That's not what they want. They want to generate leads, improve their sales conversion, automate their receptionist. They don't know the precise steps to take for their specific use case, nor do they have the support of anyone who's actually done it. So they're left with AI goals they can't execute, and every month they fall further behind someone who figured it out.

## Graveyard — framings we explicitly reject

### 🪦 Graveyard 1 — "AI Education / Upskilling Platform"
Anchors on *knowledge*, not *outcomes*. "How to use AI" beats "AI course" by 2.5×. 1,704 mentions of courses going stale. Puts us next to Coursera ("structured but slow, outdated by completion"), Maven ("expensive, cohort timing"), Udemy ("cheap but quality lottery").

### 🪦 Graveyard 2 — "AI Coaching / AI Career Coach"
"Coaching" connotes wellness / talk, not shipping. 909 mentions want "hands-on, for MY situation" — they want a practitioner, not a coach. "AI coaching" has search demand (21.7 Trends avg) useful for SEO capture, but the product is mentorship not coaching.

### 🪦 Graveyard 3 — "AI Mentorship Marketplace"
(1) "AI mentor" suggests AI *is* the mentor. (2) "Marketplace" puts us next to Upwork ("outsourcing instead of learning"), Mentorcruise, GrowthMentor. 359 mentions of guru/trust skepticism confirm the market is filtering against marketplace dynamics.

### 🪦 Graveyard 4 — "AI Productivity Tool / Workflow Automation"
Positions us as a tool, not a service. 830 people already tried ChatGPT direct and hit the wall. 332 say tools "without your business context are basically useless." The gap isn't the tool — it's the human who helps you apply it to YOUR context.
