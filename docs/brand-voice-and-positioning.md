## The name is the brand idea

**illuminairy** = *illuminate* + *luminary* + the discoverable **AI** in the wordmark.

### Illuminate

To **illuminate** (Merriam-Webster):

1. **Supply physical light** — brighten a dark area.
2. **Make something clear** — explain until a hard subject is understandable.

**What we do:** Mentors **shed light on tough concepts**, **clarify the path**, and **guide** so families are not lost in opaque prep or generic worksheets.

### Luminary

A **luminary** is someone who **inspires others through achievement** in a field—a leading light. Mentors are **near-peer luminaries**: close enough to relate, proven enough to trust (e.g. 1450+ SAT, selective-university path).

### Story → expression

| Idea | Brand expression |
|------|------------------|
| **Light** | Clarity, guidance, relief from confusion—not harsh or cramped (see [visual-identity.md](visual-identity.md) for color and layout) |
| **Clarity** | Plain language; transparent progress; path visible |
| **Path** | Steps from current level → goal; not endless content |
| **Beside you** | We **walk with you**—mentor + program, not passive video |

**Line:** *Your guiding light for goals and growth.*

**Promise:** We **illuminate** the path and **guide** you along it—with mentors who have already been where you're going.

### Frame A — Umbrella

> For ambitious high achievers from high school through professional careers, illuminairy is near-peer mentorship that pairs them with mentors who have already achieved the same goal they're pursuing—using an approach continuously grounded in the latest research to guide them through completion.

### Frame B — Parents · SAT · selective colleges

> For parents of ambitious high schoolers targeting admission to selective universities, illuminairy pairs your child with current undergrad mentors who scored 1450+ on the SAT from selective universities in the tier your family is aiming for—using an approach continuously grounded in the latest research to keep them on track through the SAT and admissions path.

**Language choices locked in workshop:**

- Say **selective universities** (not "top 100" unless legally reviewed).
- Say **research grounded in the latest evidence** / **research-informed**—not legacy playbook.
- **Do not use "structured"** in customer-facing copy. Prefer: clear plan, guided path, week-one diagnostics, weekly visibility.
- Mentors can be **near-peer and aspirational expert at once**—not either/or in messaging.

## Why we win vs in-person (full narrative from workshop)

Use for comparison tables, landing sections, sales decks. Deeper SAT-specific contrasts (guarantees, pacing, marketplaces) → [sat-messaging-positioning.md](sat-messaging-positioning.md).

### Big in-person group class

- Child is a **number**; teaching is **generic**.
- Easy to **scroll, put head down, zone out**—no one intervenes.
- Often **worksheets + volume**, not depth on weak areas.
- **No 1:1 when stuck**; no clear view of **when/why** they miss things → more content, same gaps.

### 1:1 in-person tutor

- Can still be **passive** ("session went fine") with no proof.
- Often **paper-first**—wrong for the **digital SAT**.

### illuminairy counter

- **Small live groups** (cap 10)—participation required.
- **Interactive sessions:** students work problems **aloud**, explain logic, **help a peer** get to the answer live—not background Zoom.
- **Engagement tracked and reported** to parents (not "attended 60 minutes" only).
- **Diagnostics + in-class work + exit tickets → mentor** starts next 1:1 from real data.
- **Digital-interface practice** (timing, on-screen tools, Desmos/graphing calculator fluency).

### Digital SAT — parent education (use in copy)

**Metaphor:** "You wouldn't practice soccer on a baseball field. Don't prepare for a **digital SAT** on paper."

**Teach parents:**

- Most students **run out of time**—winning is knowing **when to move on**.
- They must learn **built-in formulas, references, on-screen calculator**—which tool, when, for speed + accuracy.
- That fluency is best built on the **same kind of interface** as test day—**online program**, not a workbook franchise.

### Session engagement (product truth for copy—confirm what's live)

Parents fear **fake attendance** (kid on Zoom scrolling). Our model should **read** as:

- **Live turns** every session—work problems, talk through reasoning.
- **Peer guide** moments—student helps another reach the answer.
- **Participation expectations** (e.g. camera on, no hiding in lecture back row).
- **Exit ticket / gate** (e.g. minimum questions asked / checkpoints)—*only claim in marketing if shipped*.
- Optional: focus/browser expectations—*only if shipped*.

**Loop to show in diagrams:**

```text
Diagnostics + class participation + exit tickets + practice
        ↓
Mentor sees last group + homework performance
        ↓
Next 1:1 targets gaps — not "next generic worksheet"
```

## Voice & copy (locked)

### Rules

- **Plain, direct language.** No corp-speak. No jargon.
- **Outcomes over features.** **People over process.**
- **Not overly salesy** or emotional. **Not too cold.** Warmth via **honesty and clarity**.
- **Educate** briefly (digital SAT, engagement)—don't lecture.
- **No guaranteed scores or admission promises.** Honest about effort and progress.

### Do / don't examples

| Don't | Do |
|-------|-----|
| "Leverage our holistic ecosystem to optimize outcomes." | "The SAT is digital. We practice on the real interface—and you'll see how your student's doing, not just that they logged on." |
| "Transform your future!!!" | "We don't promise a score. We show you the plan, the effort, and the progress every week." |
| "12-week cohort-based synchronous modules…" | "Twelve weeks: diagnostics, live classes, six 1:1s, practice every week—so gaps get fixed, not buried." |

### Words to use

guide · path · clear · show · see · on track · mentor · student · family · research · progress · effort · selective · digital · practice

### Words to avoid (marketing)

leverage · solution · ecosystem · transform · unlock (unless literal) · synergy · holistic · best-in-class · world-class · cutting-edge · revolutionary · **structured** (customer-facing) · guaranteed score/admission language

### Anti-tone (never sound like)

stuffy · vague · scammy · cramped · unguided · legacy worksheet factory · passive Zoom · hype without proof · big-lecture prep hall

### Channel tone

| Surface | Dial |
|---------|------|
| **Parent site, reports, enrollment** | **Airy, easy, clear, reassuring**—reduce anxiety |
| **Student-facing** | Slightly more **playful, colorful, youthful**—still direct |
| **Investor / platform** | Confident, spare, ambition visible—still not cold |

### NN/g tone targets (UX copy)

| Dimension | Target (~1–7) |
|-----------|----------------|
| Funny ↔ Serious | **~3** |
| Formal ↔ Casual | **~5–6** |
| Respectful ↔ Irreverent | **~2–3** |
| Enthusiastic ↔ Matter-of-fact | **~3–4** |

## Proof & promises (workshop)

| Stage | What we say |
|-------|-------------|
| **Now** | **Research-informed** methodology; educate on digital SAT + engagement + diagnostics loop |
| **After programs complete** | Real **testimonials** (permissioned) |
| **When documented** | **Before/after** progress (honest framing, effort thresholds) |
| **Guarantees** | Decide **after data + legal**—prefer **commitments** until then |

### Commitments (OK now—not score guarantees)

- Weekly **visibility** for parents (effort, engagement, progress).
- **Mentor bar** (1450+, selective-tier path, vetted).
- **Program as described** (12 weeks, 2 classes, 6 1:1s, diagnostics week one).
- **Live participation** model—not lecture-hall anonymity.
- **Honesty:** we won't promise a number; we **will** show the plan and the work.

### Sales line (until outcome stats exist)

> "We don't put a guaranteed score on the website—that's not honest. We commit to how we run the program, who teaches, how sessions work, and what we report to you every week. As programs finish, we'll publish real outcomes."

**Why we don't offer score guarantees (long form):** industry patterns, consultation scripts, and parent-facing explanation → [sat-messaging-positioning.md](sat-messaging-positioning.md) § Pillar B.
